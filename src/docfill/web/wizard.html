The standard documents to fill; choose several to fill them from the same data (e.g. Anexa 2a and Anexa 4). They are used exactly as stored; only their fields are filled in.

2. Source documents

Where the information comes from: identity card, birth certificate, an already filled form… PDF, Word (.docx), PNG or JPEG. Photos and scans are read with OCR.
