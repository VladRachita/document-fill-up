How docfill becomes an expert

1. Knowledge you feedLegal forms, procedures (open / change / close), legal rules and document types, written as YAML. Every change is versioned and kept in the history.
2. Verified by peopleEverything starts as draft. A lawyer or expert checks it and marks it verified; changing it makes it draft again.
3. Laws you feedUpload the text of a law or an ONRC guide: it is split into articles, searchable here, and shown next to the rules that cite it.
4. Learning from filesEvery file saved in the wizard teaches the document classifier and records which rules were overridden, so wrong or outdated rules surface for review.
